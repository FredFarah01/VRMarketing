The challenge

Recruiting Care Staff Shouldn't Mean Chasing Paperwork

Care recruitment can involve applications, employment histories, references, DBS checks, Right to Work evidence, safeguarding assessments and numerous supporting documents.

When those processes are spread across emails, spreadsheets and folders, it becomes harder to see what is complete, what is missing and which candidates are ready to progress.
